Hunza Valley

Famous for mountain views, heritage forts, traditional villages and the Karakoram Highway.

Places to visit
  • Baltit Fort and Karimabad
  • Altit Fort and village
  • Eagle's Nest viewpoint at Duikar
  • Attabad Lake
  • Passu Cones and Upper Hunza
  • Khunjerab Pass, with extra travel time
Approximate distances
Gilgit to Karimabad: 100 km · Karimabad to Attabad Lake: around 30–40 km

Suggested stay: 2–4 days

Good for: Heritage, village walks, photography and lake activities where available.

Shandur Pass

A high-altitude plateau known for its polo ground, wide meadows and mountain scenery.

Places to visit
  • Shandur Polo Ground

Travel Tips & Local Knowledge

Practical Guides for Your Visit

Use these guides to choose a destination, prepare for the journey and decide what to ask about before booking local services.

🏔️ Guide to Gilgit

Gilgit is a useful base for acclimatizing to the region and planning onward road trips.

  • Allow time for the bazaar and local food.
  • Arrange transport in advance for Naltar and check road conditions.
  • Ask about a local guide for historical sites and nearby valleys.
  • Keep a flexible schedule for mountain weather.

🏰 Guide to Hunza

Hunza rewards visitors who allow enough time for the forts, villages and longer drive north.

  • Visit Baltit Fort and Altit Fort with time to explore their surroundings.
  • Consider an overnight stay in Upper Hunza if visiting Passu.
  • Ask about boat availability and safety at Attabad Lake.
  • Khunjerab Pass is a longer excursion; check access and travel requirements.

🏞️ Guide to Skardu

Plan Skardu around the lakes and nearby valleys rather than trying to fit every attraction into one day.

  • Group nearby lakes and viewpoints to reduce driving time.
  • Ask about a suitable vehicle for Shigar and Deosai trips.
  • Deosai is high altitude and seasonal; verify access before departing.
  • Pack warm layers even when the weather in town is mild.

🌿 Guide to Ghizer

Ghizer is suited to slower travel, river scenery and exploring villages along the route.

  • Allow extra time for photo stops and mountain roads.
  • Discuss accommodation in advance for smaller valleys.
  • Ask local hosts about suitable walks and community customs.
  • Confirm fuel, transport and mobile connectivity for remote trips.

💧 Guide to Phander

Phander is a good choice for visitors who want a quieter stay surrounded by nature.

  • Consider staying overnight rather than rushing back to Gilgit.
  • Ask your accommodation about meals and local transport.
  • Respect private land and local fishing rules.
  • Bring suitable clothing for cool evenings.

🐎 Guide to Shandur Pass

Shandur is a high-altitude destination where weather and road access are especially important.

  • Plan travel in the usual summer access season and verify current conditions.
  • Allow generous time for the road journey and stops.
  • Book accommodation and transport early during polo festival dates.
  • Bring warm clothing, sun protection, water and essential medicines.
